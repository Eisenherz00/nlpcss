# Ideas from Caro

## 3. Development of requests for an answer from assertions

* **Input (real world):** Assertion from step 2.
* **Input (evaluation):** List of assertions covering all basic concepts with at least three examples.
* **Agent task:** Take the assertion and make it a question (including answer options).

---

## Evaluation Criteria

*(Scale: 1 = very subjective to 5 = very objective)*

### 1. Question Format (Score: 5)
**Question(s) for evaluation:** Which format is chosen?
* Direct interrogative with or without a WH word
* Direct imperative
* Indirect interrogative
* Indirect imperative

**Example:**
*Assertion: “I am satisfied with my job”*
* **Direct interrogative with or without a WH word:** Are you satisfied with your job? How satisfied are you with your job?
* **Direct imperative:** Indicate your satisfaction with your job by a number between 0 and 10, where 0 means not at all satisfied and 10 means completely satisfied.
* **Indirect interrogative:** Do you feel that you are satisfied with your job?
* **Indirect imperative:** Please tell me how satisfied you are with your job?

### 2. Assertion–question alignment (Score: 4)
**Question(s) for evaluation:** Does the question accurately represent the assertion from the previous step?

### Detailed Checks

| Question to check | Category | (Bad) Example | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Leading question yes/no** | Wording question | *Don’t you agree that stricter environmental regulations are necessary?* | Wording suggests a preferred answer |
| **Loaded questions** | Wording question | *What is the best book you read last year?* | Requests with implicit assumptions |
| **Recall error** | Wording question | *How many hours did you spend on social media in the last month?* | Respondents cannot accurately remember past behavior. |
| **Vague or ambiguous wording** | Wording question | *How often do you exercise regularly?* | key concepts are undefined or subjective |
| **Sensible topic** | Wording question | *Are you prejudiced against transgender people?* vs *Do you think that prejudice vs transgender people is…* | alternatives like indirect question, images or letters (on showcards); self-completion mode; normalising the behaviour; graduation; highlighting the importance of the study and hence honesty; order and context of questions |
| **Open/closed question** | Scale | *What is your favorite beer?* | Open questions good if we want to know what is top of the mind, otherwise choose closed |
| **Unbalanced response categories** | Scale | *Very satisfied / satisfied / somewhat satisfied / dissatisfied* | Answer options favor one side of the scale |
| **Order labels** | Scale | | First positive, then negative or vice versa (recommended) |
| **Before categories** | Scale | | Numbers/Letters/Nothing |
| **Scale polarity** | Scale | | Unipolar/Bipolar (recommended if bipolar concept) |
| **Labels for numbers** | Scale | | Are the points of the scale labeled (recommended) |
| **Number of points** | Scale | | Not more than 11 recommended (some say 7 or 5) |
| **Agree-disagree** | Scale | *Would you say it is generally bad or good for [country]’s economy that people come to live here from other countries?* | Recommendation: Avoid agree disagree scales |
